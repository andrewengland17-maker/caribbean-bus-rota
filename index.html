How it works

👥
One helper per journey One Caribbean helper accompanies the children on each morning and afternoon run.
📆
Helper schedule Each helper does 5 shifts per fortnight. Athena only does afternoon pickups, the other helpers rotate.
🎒
Equipment Remember to bring the school bags and Ridesafer vests.
☀️
Morning pickup: 7:40am Be at the Cheers shop in the B1 car park by 7:30am. Bus departs at 7:40am sharp.
🚌
Bus 97 for helpers After morning drop-off you are dropped at the 97 bus stop around 8:30am. Live departures can be found here.

Make your own way back to school by 3:00pm for afternoon shifts.
🌆
Afternoon collection: from 3:00pm Make your own way to DUCKS by 3:00pm. Collect DUCKS kids at 3:20pm, walk to PAC Building and wait for Rory & Saria at 3:30pm. Once all kids collected, call the bus via WhatsApp. Aim to leave ~3:40pm, back at condo ~4:10pm.
🔄
Swaps If you can't make your shift, please arrange a swap with another helper and let the group know 24 hours in advance.
